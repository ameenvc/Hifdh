Hifdh privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy describes Hifdh version 1.0.1 and later. Older previews may not include these age controls; update before children use voice features. It covers Hifdh, the Quran reading and memorization app, its Android preview and its website. Contact the app maintainer at info@setilas.com.

Accounts, advertising and payments

Hifdh has no app accounts, advertisements, purchases or analytics SDK. We do not operate a server that stores your progress, speech recordings or practice history. The app does not sell personal data.

Information stored on your device

Settings, bookmarks, memorized-ayah marks, practice duration, recognized-text comparison records and your selected age band are stored locally. Review records contain text recognized during recitation, not audio recordings. We ask for an age range to choose privacy settings, not your date of birth. The age choice is not sent to an app-owned server.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app requests microphone permission when you start recitation. Capture stops when you pause or leave the app. You can read, use meanings, hints and manual memorization without allowing microphone access.

For users under 18, users who prefer not to answer the age question, and users with no age choice, native voice is restricted to the operating system's on-device recognition API. There is no cloud-recognition fallback in this mode. Supported Arabic models may not be installed or available; if unavailable, use manual study. Restricted-mode voice is unavailable in the browser because the app cannot guarantee local-only browser processing.

Adults who select the 18-or-older age band can use the device or browser speech service, which may send live audio to its provider for processing. That provider, rather than Hifdh, controls its service, transport, processing and retention. The app does not save an audio file. Recognition can mishear correct recitation; this app is not a validated Quran pronunciation or tajweed assessor.

Online reference audio and outside links

For adult profiles, reference recitation audio is streamed from cdn.islamic.network. This provider receives the requested audio URL and normal network information such as an IP address. Outside source links open other services with their own policies. Online reference audio and external links are disabled for under-18 and unanswered/prefer-not age profiles. Source and licence notices are bundled in the native app.

Children and shared devices

Children can use bundled Quran reading, meanings, grammar, colours, hints and manual memorization. The app does not offer ads, public messaging or profiles. It does not choose cloud speech processing for restricted profiles. An age-band choice is self-declared and is not a claim of verified parental consent. On shared devices, review Age & privacy options before starting a session. Parents can contact info@setilas.com with questions.

Website and service providers

The public website and downloads are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the website makes ordinary network requests to the host, which may retain technical access logs under its own policy. The native app bundles Quran text, fonts, grammar and meanings, so normal reading does not require fetching those files from the website. Device speech providers and online audio services have their own processing and retention practices. HTTPS is used for the app's web and audio URLs; we cannot make a universal encryption or deletion guarantee about every operating-system speech provider.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on the device until removed. Delete individual attempts in Review, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Hifdh → Storage. Uninstalling removes local app data. For the website, clear this site's browser data. There is no app account to delete. Changing Age & privacy options stops an active session and changes subsequent access to online features. Revoking microphone permission prevents new voice sessions. Data retained by a speech/hosting/audio provider is subject to that provider's own policies; we do not have access to delete its records.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or help deleting local records, contact info@setilas.com. We will update this policy and its effective date when the app's relevant processing changes.
